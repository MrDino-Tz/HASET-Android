# 📛 Required Badges & Certifications
## HASET Digital Health Application

To build trust and comply with Google Play standards, the following badges and certifications are required or recommended for the official launch.

---

### 1. Store Recognition Badges
*   **"Get it on Google Play" Badge**: This is the official marketing badge for your website and social media.
    *   *Usage*: Must follow Google's [Brand Guidelines](https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/).
    *   *Requirement*: Use the official localized versions (e.g., in English or Swahili).

*   **"Designed for Tablets" Badge**: Google automatically grants this once you upload screenshots for 7-inch and 10-inch tablets.
    *   *Benefit*: Improves visibility in the "Tablet-optimized" section of the store.

*   **"Editors' Choice" or "Teacher Approved"**: (Long-term) These are awarded by Google for high-quality apps.

---

### 2. Medical & Security Badges (Recommended for UI)
To reassure patients and doctors about data safety, we recommend displaying these icons in the **"About App"** or **"Data Safety"** section:

*   **🔐 SSL/TLS Encrypted**: Indicates that all communication between the app and the server is secure.
*   **🛡️ Secure Data Storage**: Confirms that patient records are stored using encrypted databases.
*   **✅ Verified Medical Content**: Used to sign off on the accuracy of the "Health Articles" feature.

---

### 3. Content & Compliance Labels
Google will assign these based on your submission questionnaire:

*   **PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone**: Content rating for medical apps (no mature content).
*   **"Contains Ads" Label**: (Ensure this is set to NO, as the app is for medical service).
*   **"In-app Purchases" Label**: Required if you enable paid consultations or pharmacy orders directly.

---

### 4. Developer Badges (Google Play Console)
*   **Verified Developer**: Shown after providing government ID and DUNS number.
*   **App Status**: 
    *   `Internal Testing` (Badge shown to testers).
    *   `Top Developer` (Awarded based on performance).
